## 3. NEW Findings

### [P1] Modern building completely hides the player — no occlusion translucency

- **Where:** `Game.cpp:3774+` (branch `case 20`, modern building, opaque `DrawCube`).
  Translucent occluder handling **exists and works** — but only in the `.obj`
  model branch (`Game.cpp:3486-3491`, alpha 0.30 when `odz∈(0,620) and odx<230`).
  The modern building, which dominates ALL urban zones, never goes through it.
- **Evidence (MEASURED, 2 seeds):** `shot_01` from run seed 7 **and** `shot_01` from run
  seed 20260821 (both t≈60 s, phase 1): the player is behind the slab of a building
  covering >60% of the screen; the only clue of position is the skill glow. The hero is
  unlocatable — fails the half-second readability test.
- **Repro:** `--autotest --test-seconds=90 --seed=7`, look at shot_01 (t≈60 s).
- **Player effect:** loses sight of the character in the middle of combat every time
  the route passes south of a building; in an urban phase this is constant. It is
  exactly the problem the code's own comment describes — solved for the rare models
  and not for the ubiquitous occluder.
- **Cost of not fixing:** recurring frustration in the FIRST phase of the game
  (first impression), "unfair" death without seeing the character.
- **Note (DEDUCED):** even in the covered branch, the fixed window (620/230) does not
  scale with the real footprint of the occluder — W≈250–330u and height up to
  ~470u buildings occlude from farther than that.

### [P2] Dark Forest unreadable from darkness

- **Evidence (MEASURED):** `shot_07` and `shot_08` (300 s run, t≈237/267 s): outside the
  central cone, enemies and scenery disappear into pure black; in `shot_08` even the
  player has to be searched for. The 11 kills between the two frames happened
  essentially blind — the bot does not care, the player does.
- **Effect:** phase 3 becomes literally "shoot in the dark". Fog and fear work
  with silhouette and rim light; here there is no minimum actor readability.
- **Cost:** phase 3 of 11 — too early for the player to accept not seeing.

### [P2] The gate does not protect the most important achievement of this cycle

- **Where:** `BotController::passed()` (`BotController.cpp:279-291`).
- **Evidence:** criteria are FPS/stuck/death/kill/distance. **None requires `zonesVisited ≥ 1`.** If phase advancement regresses to zero tomorrow, the gate
  passes again — exactly the hole that left the central mechanic without
  coverage for the entire previous cycle.
- **Suggested acceptance:** for runs ≥ 180 s, fail with `zonesVisited == 0`.

### [P2] The "intentional guarantee" of the portal is weaker than the name suggests

- **Where:** `Game_Phases.cpp:129-139`.
- **Evidence (code):** (a) only validates the **center point** — the interaction
  disk has radius 105u and can still be 90% covered; (b) ring tests **11**
  candidates (t=1..11, not 12 as noted) on a single 700u radius;
  (c) if all blocked, **keeps the blocked position** and only logs — the
  original soft-lock becomes possible again, now with a log; (d) nothing tests
  reachability (a free but walled point passes).
- **Context:** deterministic reconstruction of audit 1 showed the corridor
  free in phase 1 (120/120), and no `PORTAL:` warning appeared in the 3 runs
  (phases 1–4). Real residual risk, not theoretical-urgent.

### [P2] `SCENERY` log reports `structures=0` in urban phase

- **Where:** `Game_WorldGen.cpp:310-318` — counter only sums types 0/1/7/8/10
  (medieval catalog); modern building (20), cars (6) and biome structures
  (14–19) are left out.
- **Evidence (MEASURED):** `SCENERY zona=Ruinas de Los Angeles total=2991
  estruturas=0` with the city visibly full of buildings in the frames. Same
  class as the FPS alert that cried wolf: a number that measures the wrong thing
  teaches you to ignore the log.

### [P2] Hardcoded absolute paths in bot reports

- **Where:** `Game.cpp:518`, `Game_Bot.cpp:116` and `Game_Bot.cpp:127` —
  `C:\Users\ricar\darknet-prototype\...` written into source.
- **Effect:** on any other machine (including future CI) the final/partial report
  silently does not output or goes to the wrong place.

### [P2] Autotest does not clean `shot_NN.png` from previous runs

- **Evidence (MEASURED):** after the 120 s run, `build/Release` contained
  new `shot_00-03` (20:30-31) mixed with `shot_04-09` from the 20:04 run
  — evidence from different runs with the same name, indistinguishable without `ls -la`.
  New trap for AUDITOR.md list.

### [P2] Inconsistent phase texts

- **Evidence (MEASURED, frames):** in phase 2 the header says `CAP.1 | Cursed
  Farm` while the phase title says `PHASE 2 - Cursed Farm` — chapter frozen at 1
  (only `transitionToZone` from the old system advances `storyChapter`; `advanceOpenWorldPhase` does not) and two names for the same place on the same screen.

### Minor observations (no severity)

- Detached screenshot encode thread (`Game_Shaders.cpp:205-208`): on a quit at the
  wrong instant, the process may exit with a half-written PNG. Affects only the instrument.
- Worst render() of 45.3 ms in the 300 s run (vs ~21-22 ms in 120 s runs) — isolated
  peak, coincides with world reconstruction frames at phase swap; gameplay FPS
  did not feel it (min 42 with 7 enemies).
