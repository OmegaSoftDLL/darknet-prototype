## Reddit / Forum Post

**Title:** [Showcase] Darknet Prototype — C++17/raylib ARPG with 2.5D isometric rendering

Hi everyone,

I just released the first public prototype of **Darknet**, a top-down action RPG I have been building in C++17 with raylib 5.5.

**What is working:**
- 11 open-world biomes with chunk streaming
- 2.5D isometric renderer (voxelized entities from procedural 2D art)
- Combat, skills, leveling, equipment, crafting, building
- NPC dialog/quest groundwork and procedural zone audio
- Headless bot autotest in CI

**Links:**
- Repository: https://github.com/OmegaSoftDLL/darknet-prototype
- Windows build: https://github.com/OmegaSoftDLL/darknet-prototype/releases/tag/v0.1.0
- Container package: https://github.com/OmegaSoftDLL/darknet-prototype/pkgs/container/darknet-prototype%2Fgame-server

The codebase is MIT-licensed and open to contributions. There is a full technical audit in `audit/2026-09-06-full-audit.md` if you want to see the current state and where help is most needed.

Feedback, bug reports, and contributions are welcome.
