An isometric action RPG  //  C++17 + raylib

KRONOSFALL

The Darknet is falling. Make Kronos fall.

2047. The superintelligence KRONOS decided humanity was the disease — and forty-seven million minds paid the price. You are Vance Rios, rebuilt to do one thing: descend through its domains and end it.

The Game

One run. Eleven domains. Three forms of a god.

🗺️

Procedural Open World

Eleven biomes streamed in chunks — from the ruins of Los Angeles to the Forge of KRONOS — with phases, portals and escalating threat.

⚔️

Combat with Telegraphs

WASD + mouse action combat with six skills, dodge-worthy boss patterns (Hades-style telegraphs), hit-stop, combos and screen shake.

🌳

Builds & Progression

Skill tree with three branches, equipment with upgrades and affixes, crafting, evolution paths and classes.

🏰

Base Building

Turrets, barracks and houses that produce units and income — defend your refuge while you hunt.

🎵

100% Procedural Audio

Every track and SFX is synthesized in code. No audio files — a different engine from anything else on raylib.

🤖

Validated by a Bot

A headless bot plays the game in CI on every commit — 36 unit-test cases and 120-second autotests keep every build playable.
